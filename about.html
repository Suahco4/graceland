We Are Grace Land

🌟 The Power of Grace Ministries International Inc. ignites hearts with healing, hope, and faith—restoring lives through grace, and embracing every soul with love and purpose. A sanctuary where God's mercy flows abundantly.

Our Purpose.

🕊️ Our purpose at The Power of Grace Ministries International Inc. is to restore lives through the Gospel of Grace—empowering individuals with healing, deliverance, and prayer, while preparing hearts for eternity and reaching the world through evangelism.

Our Mission.

🌍 Our mission at The Power of Grace Ministries International Inc. is to restore lives through the Gospel of Grace—bringing healing, deliverance, and spiritual renewal to individuals and communities, while preparing hearts for eternity and reaching the world through evangelism.

For God so loved the world, that he gave his only Son, that whoever believes in him should not perish but have eternal life. For God did not send his Son into the world to condemn the world, but in order that the world might be saved through him.

John 3:16-17 ESV

Our Values.

Identity in Christ

🙏 Our values at The Power of Grace Ministries International Inc. are the pillars that guide our mission and reflect our heart for God and His people: - Grace: We embrace the transformative power of God's grace to heal, restore, and empower every life. - Prayer: We believe in the power of prayer to connect with God, bring deliverance, and shift spiritual atmospheres. - Holiness: We strive to live in purity and obedience, honoring God in all we do. - Evangelism: We are committed to reaching the world with the Gospel, valuing every soul Christ died for. - Unity: We walk in love and humility, fostering a spirit of togetherness and mutual respect.

Church Is Family

Absolutely ❤️ Church is more than a building—it’s a home filled with grace, love, and belonging. At The Power of Grace Ministries International Inc., we cherish the beauty of spiritual family, where: - Every soul matters: You're not just a member, you're family. - Support flows freely: We lift each other up in prayer and compassion. - Joy is shared: Celebrations, testimonies, and breakthroughs become collective moments of praise. - Purpose is nurtured: Together we grow, serve, and walk in our divine calling. - Unity prevails: We embrace differences and stand united in Christ.


What We Believe.

"Restoring Lives, Reflecting Grace, Reaching Nations."

🙏 We believe in one eternal God, salvation through Jesus Christ, the power of grace, the authority of Scripture, the work of the Holy Spirit, and the church as God's family—called to reflect His kingdom on earth.
