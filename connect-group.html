Connect Groups

Life is not meant to be lived alone. At The Power of Grace Ministries, we believe that spiritual growth and true fellowship happen in community. Our Connect Groups are small, welcoming gatherings where you can build authentic relationships, study God’s Word together, and support one another through life’s joys and challenges.

Joining a Connect Group is the best way to get plugged into the heart of our church family. It’s a place to be known, encouraged, and challenged as you walk out your faith. Whether you're new to the faith or have been a believer for years, there’s a group for you. Find your place, find your people, and let's do life together.
