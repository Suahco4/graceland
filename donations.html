Support Our Ministry

Your generous support helps us continue our mission to spread the Gospel of Grace, restore lives, and serve our community. Every contribution, no matter the size, makes a significant impact and allows us to bring hope and healing to those in need.

Ways to Give

We are grateful for your willingness to partner with us. You can give in the way that is most convenient for you.

Mobile Money

Giving is simple with Mobile Money. You can send your contribution directly from your phone using the details below.

  • Lonestar Cell MTN: 088-XXX-XXXX (Your Name/Ministry Name)
  • Orange Money: 077-XXX-XXXX (Your Name/Ministry Name)

Bank Transfer

You can give directly through a bank transfer to our ministry's account. Please use the following details.

  • Bank Name: Your Bank Name (e.g., Ecobank Liberia)
  • Account Name: The Power Of Grace Ministries
  • Account Number (LRD): Your LRD Account Number
  • Account Number (USD): Your USD Account Number
  • Swift Code/BIC: Your Bank's Swift Code

Secure Online Giving

For international members and partners, you can use our secure online portal to give via card. This is a safe and easy way to support our mission from anywhere in the world.
