Sunday Worship Service

Join us every Sunday for a powerful time of worship, prayer, and an inspiring message from the Word of God. It's a perfect opportunity to connect with the community, experience the presence of the Holy Spirit, and grow in your faith. Whether you are a long-time believer or just exploring Christianity, you are welcome here.

Our services are filled with vibrant worship, heartfelt prayer, and relevant, Bible-based teaching that you can apply to your daily life. We also have a dedicated children's ministry to ensure your little ones are cared for and are learning about Jesus in a fun and safe environment. Come and be part of our family as we celebrate the goodness of God together.
