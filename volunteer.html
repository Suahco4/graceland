Join the Team

Serving is at the heart of what we do at The Power of Grace Ministries. We believe that using our God-given gifts to serve others is one of the most fulfilling ways to live out our faith and build His kingdom. When you volunteer, you're not just filling a role—you're becoming a vital part of a team dedicated to spreading love, hope, and grace.

There are many opportunities to get involved, and every role is essential to the health and growth of our church family. Whether you have a passion for welcoming new faces, mentoring children, leading worship, or working behind the scenes, there is a place for you here. Join us in making a difference, one act of service at a time.


Volunteer With Us Through the Following Ministries

Ushering & Security.

Be the first to welcome members and guests with a warm smile. This team helps create a friendly and safe atmosphere by assisting with seating, collecting offerings, and ensuring the service flows smoothly.

Kids Church.

Invest in the next generation by teaching children about God's love in a fun, engaging, and safe environment. If you have a heart for kids, this is the perfect place to serve.

Music Team.

Use your musical talents to lead the congregation in worship. We are looking for skilled vocalists and instrumentalists with a passion for creating an atmosphere of praise.

Prayer Team.

Stand in the gap for our church, community, and nation. This team is dedicated to intercessory prayer, believing for breakthroughs, healing, and God's guidance in all things.

Administrative Support.

Help keep the ministry running smoothly from behind the scenes. This team assists with office tasks, data entry, and organizational support that is crucial for our day-to-day operations.

Production Design.

Bring your creative vision to life by helping design our stage and visual elements for services and special events. This team creates an inspiring and welcoming atmosphere for worship.

Technical & Stage Management.

Ensure our services run seamlessly by managing sound, lighting, and media presentations. If you are tech-savvy and detail-oriented, this team is a great fit for you.

Communications.

Help share the story of what God is doing at our church. This team manages our website, social media, and other communication channels to keep our community informed and engaged.
